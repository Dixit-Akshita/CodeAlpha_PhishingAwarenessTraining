# 🔐 Phishing Awareness Training

A cybersecurity awareness presentation focused on identifying, understanding, and preventing phishing attacks.

This project was created as **Task 2** of my **Cyber Security Internship at CodeAlpha**.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Phishing is one of the most common forms of cyber attack where attackers use deception and social engineering to trick users into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.

This project provides a beginner-friendly phishing awareness training covering common phishing techniques, warning signs, fake websites, social engineering tactics, prevention methods, and incident response.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Understand what phishing is and how it works
* Learn how to identify suspicious phishing emails
* Recognize fake websites and suspicious URLs
* Understand social engineering techniques
* Learn about different types of phishing attacks
* Follow best practices to avoid phishing attacks
* Understand what to do after accidentally interacting with a phishing attack
* Test awareness through interactive quiz questions

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. What is Phishing?

Introduction to phishing attacks, their purpose, and common targets.

### 2. Phishing Attack Workflow

A basic explanation of how attackers use bait, impersonation, urgency, and malicious links or attachments.

### 3. Phishing Emails

Common warning signs such as suspicious senders, urgent messages, unexpected attachments, and requests for sensitive information.

### 4. Fake Websites & Suspicious URLs

How attackers create convincing fake websites and how users can inspect domains and URLs.

### 5. Social Engineering

Common psychological manipulation techniques including:

* Urgency
* Fear
* Rewards
* Impersonation
* Authority

### 6. Types of Phishing

* Phishing
* Spear Phishing
* Whaling
* Smishing
* Vishing

### 7. Real-World Example

A brief discussion of the **2020 Twitter account compromise** and the role of social engineering.

### 8. Prevention & Best Practices

Practical security measures including:

* Checking senders and URLs
* Using strong and unique passwords
* Enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* Keeping software updated
* Avoiding unexpected attachments and links
* Verifying suspicious requests through official channels

### 9. Incident Response

Steps to take after accidentally clicking a phishing link or submitting sensitive information.

### 10. Interactive Quiz

Short scenario-based questions designed to test phishing awareness.

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

The training emphasizes five important security habits:

**STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → PROTECT → REPORT**

* **STOP** before reacting to unexpected messages.
* **CHECK** the sender and links.
* **VERIFY** requests through official channels.
* **PROTECT** accounts using strong passwords and MFA.
* **REPORT** suspicious messages or security incidents.

> **Think before you click. A few seconds of caution can prevent a serious security incident.**
